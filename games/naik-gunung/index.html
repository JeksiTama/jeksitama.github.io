HOW TO PLAY

🎲

Roll the die and fly that many tiles up the mountain.

📝

Then answer a question. Right: fly on. Wrong or too slow: stay put. Or PASS.

🎯

Launch pads fling you up. Land on a spring and fly to the target with its number.

🌊

Rivers wash you down from one pond to another. But every river you ride makes your right answers fly 1 tile further, for the rest of the match.

🎯

Know the answer? Then you pick where you land: answer, or PASS to dodge a river.

⛰️

First plane to tile 64 at the top wins. Everyone puts in . First takes 4, last takes none, the rest get theirs back.

⚙️

Special rules like duels and rainy season are in /settings/.
