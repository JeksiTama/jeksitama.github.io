HOW TO PLAY

📝

Help Bu Guru mark one student's test. They shaded one answer in pencil for each question.

✓

Right answer? Draw a tick on the paper.

✗

Wrong answer? Draw a cross.

?

Don't know? Draw a question mark to skip it.

💥

3 wrong marks and you're fired.

📘

At the end the test gets your score, and Bu Guru checks it.

🪙

Work once a day: After that you can volunteer, just for fun.

⌨️

Keyboard: T for a tick, X for a cross, ? to skip.
